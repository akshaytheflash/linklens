How it works

Every scan is a real visit, not a guess

LinkLens loads the page in an isolated Chromium and instruments everything that happens.

01

Run the page

A headless browser visits the URL with rotated fingerprints. Downloads, popups, dialogs and network calls are captured in real time.

02

Inspect deeply

WHOIS age, SSL certificates, redirect chains, hidden iframes, password exfiltration, YARA malware rules and page structure are all examined.

03

Score the risk

An optional AI review weighs the evidence and every finding is combined into a 0–10 risk score with a plain-language breakdown.

What you get

Four layers of protection

Sandboxed browsing

The link opens in a real, isolated Chromium with downloads, popups and network calls watched live.

AI review

Gemini reads the URL, page text and popup content and scores how scammy it looks. Works without it too.

Deep inspection

WHOIS age, SSL certs, redirect chains, YARA malware rules, hidden iframes and password exfiltration attempts.

Live behavior

Redirects are followed and re-checked, fingerprinting attempts flagged, and every console error preserved in the report.

See it before you trust it

Every report includes a screenshot of the final page, a 0–10 risk score, and a plain-language explanation of why the verdict was reached.
